ABOUT ME

My Journey in Front-End Development

My interest in Front-End Development started during my studies, where I first learned programming through C++. I enjoyed writing code and solving problems, but I often felt that something was missing — I could write the logic, but I could not easily see the result of what I had created.

When I started learning Front-End Development, I found a different kind of experience. I could immediately see the impact of every change I made, from the layout and colors to the way a website looks and feels. This made the learning process more enjoyable and motivated me to explore web development further.

Today, I am building my skills in Front-End Development and working on practical projects to turn ideas into responsive, user-friendly websites. My goal is to keep learning, improve my skills, and create websites that provide a clear and enjoyable experience for users.
